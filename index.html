How to Play

QuoteDecoder is a “substitution cipher” encryption puzzle. A quote has been encrypted by swapping every letter of the alphabet with another letter. Select a square and choose a letter from the keyboard to fill it in. When all letters are correct, the full quote and author will be revealed! Click the light bulb button to fill in a letter if you need help.
